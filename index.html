OUR STORY

A good haircut is more than a cut.

Prime Cut started with a simple idea: give people a place where great barbering, good conversation and genuine attention come together.

Our barbers combine classic techniques with modern styles to create cuts that suit you — not just the latest trend. Whether you're getting ready for a big meeting, a night out or simply keeping your look sharp, we take the same care every time.
