PRIME CUT BARBER STUDIO

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This policy explains how the Prime Cut Barber Studio demonstration website handles information entered through its appointment booking experience.

1. Information you provide

When making an appointment, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, selected service, barber, appointment date and time, and optional appointment notes.

2. How booking information is used

The information is used to create and display the appointment, prevent conflicting bookings for the selected barber, generate the booking reference, and prepare optional calendar event details.

3. Browser storage

This recruitment-assessment website is a front-end demonstration. Booking records are stored in the browser's local storage on the device being used. They are not submitted to a live Prime Cut server or shared booking database.

4. Calendar features

If you choose Google Calendar, the selected appointment details are used to prepare a Google Calendar event link. If you choose Apple / Calendar, the website creates an ICS calendar file on your device. Calendar providers process information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Discount codes

A discount code entered in the booking popup is checked in the browser. If valid, the applicable discount and final price are stored with the local booking record.

6. External links

The website may link to maps, social platforms and calendar services. Those third-party services have their own privacy practices and terms.

7. Your choices

Because this demonstration stores booking records locally in your browser, clearing this site's browser storage will remove locally stored booking records. Do not enter sensitive information in the optional appointment-notes field.

8. Contact

Questions about this demonstration privacy policy can be directed to hello@primecut.co.za or +27 72 123 4567.
